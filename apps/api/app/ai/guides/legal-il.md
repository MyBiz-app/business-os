# Legal guide, Israel (draft summary)

Status: written by the MyBiz team from public official sources as a starting point. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Always check the source named in each section and send anything important to a real lawyer.

## Privacy: the Protection of Privacy Law (חוק הגנת הפרטיות)

The Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, governs databases of personal information. Amendment 13 to the law was passed in 2024 and took effect in August 2025: it widens the definition of personal information, changes the duty to register databases, adds duties to appoint a privacy protection officer in some cases, and gives the Privacy Protection Authority stronger enforcement powers and financial sanctions. Which duties apply to a given business depends on the size and the kind of its databases. Source: Privacy Protection Authority, https://www.gov.il/en/departments/the_privacy_protection_authority/govil-landing-page

## Privacy: data security regulations (תקנות אבטחת מידע)

The Privacy Protection Regulations (Data Security), 5777-2017, require a database owner to protect its information in proportion to the sensitivity and size of the database: access control, logging, backups, a written security policy, and reporting severe security incidents to the Authority. Source: Privacy Protection Authority.

## Privacy: the right to inspect and correct (זכות עיון)

A person may inspect information held about them in a database and ask to correct or delete wrong information. The business must answer in a reasonable time. Source: Protection of Privacy Law, chapter on the right of inspection, via the Privacy Protection Authority.

## Consumer: cancelling a distance transaction (ביטול עסקה)

Under the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, section 14C, a consumer may cancel a distance transaction (online or by phone) within 14 days of the transaction or of receiving the document with the details, whichever is later. The business may charge a cancellation fee of up to 5 percent of the price or 100 NIS, whichever is lower. Different rules apply to services that start within the period and to some products. Source: Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority, https://www.gov.il/he/departments/the_consumer_protection_and_fair_trade_authority/govil-landing-page

## Consumer: standard contracts (חוזים אחידים)

Terms of service that the business writes once and uses for all customers are a standard contract under the Standard Contracts Law, 5743-1982. A court or the Standard Contracts Tribunal may cancel or change unfair terms. Keep terms clear and do not hide important terms. Source: Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority.

## Marketing messages: the spam provision (ספאם, סעיף 30א)

Section 30A of the Communications (Telecommunications and Broadcasting) Law, 5742-1982, forbids sending advertising by email, SMS, automatic calling system or fax without the recipient's prior express consent in writing (an electronic message counts). There is a limited exception for an existing customer, for similar products, if the customer was offered a refusal option at the time of the sale. Every advertising message must say clearly that it is an advertisement, name the sender, and offer an easy way to unsubscribe; the business must honour an unsubscribe request. Damages may be awarded without proof of loss. Source: the text of the law on the Knesset and Ministry of Justice sites; the Consumer Protection and Fair Trade Authority publishes guidance.

## Accessibility (נגישות)

The Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Accessibility of Services Regulations, 5773-2013, require businesses that serve the public to make their services accessible, including websites and apps. The Israeli standard is IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA. A public accessibility statement and a named accessibility coordinator are required in many cases. Source: Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities, https://www.gov.il/he/departments/the_commission_for_equal_rights_of_persons_with_disabilities/govil-landing-page

## What this guide does not cover

Employment law, contract disputes, intellectual property, company law and court procedure are not in this library. For these, say that the library has nothing, and prepare questions for a lawyer.
