# Accounting and tax guide, Israel (draft summary)

Status: written by the MyBiz team from public official sources as a starting point. Not yet reviewed by an accountant. Rates and thresholds change: always check the current figure on the Israel Tax Authority site (https://www.gov.il/he/departments/israel_tax_authority/govil-landing-page) and send anything important to a real accountant (רואה חשבון).

## VAT (מע"מ)

Value added tax is charged on most sales of goods and services by a licensed dealer (עוסק מורשה). The standard rate was raised from 17 percent to 18 percent on 1 January 2025. A licensed dealer charges VAT on sales, deducts VAT paid on business expenses that are properly documented, and reports and pays the difference, monthly or every two months, depending on turnover. Source: Israel Tax Authority.

## Exempt dealer (עוסק פטור)

A small business below an annual turnover ceiling may register as an exempt dealer (עוסק פטור): it does not charge VAT and cannot deduct VAT on expenses. The ceiling is updated every year, so check the current amount on the Tax Authority site. Source: Israel Tax Authority.

## Tax invoices and allocation numbers (חשבונית מס, מספר הקצאה)

A licensed dealer issues a tax invoice (חשבונית מס) to a business customer and a tax invoice or receipt as the law requires. Under the invoice reform, an invoice above a threshold must be issued with an allocation number (מספר הקצאה) from the Tax Authority's system, otherwise the customer cannot deduct the VAT. The threshold falls in stages: check the current threshold. Invoices must be numbered in an unbroken sequence. Source: Israel Tax Authority.

## Keeping books (ניהול פנקסי חשבונות)

A business must keep books and documents as the Income Tax (Book-keeping) Regulations require, and keep them for seven years. Records may be kept electronically if the system meets the Tax Authority's requirements. Source: Israel Tax Authority.

## Income tax and the annual report (דוח שנתי)

Self-employed people and companies report income to the Tax Authority, make advance payments (מקדמות) during the year, and file an annual report. The corporate tax rate is 23 percent. An individual's tax follows tax brackets that are updated by law. The due date of the annual report is usually extended: check the current date. Source: Israel Tax Authority.

## Employees: payroll deductions and National Insurance (ביטוח לאומי)

An employer deducts income tax and employee National Insurance and health insurance contributions from each salary, pays the employer's share, and reports and pays to the Tax Authority and the National Insurance Institute. Mandatory pension contributions apply to employees by the extension order. Payroll rates and brackets change by year: use the rates table that the accountant confirms in MyBiz. Source: Israel Tax Authority and the National Insurance Institute.

## Withholding at source (ניכוי מס במקור)

A business that pays another business for services may have to withhold tax at source, unless the recipient holds a valid withholding exemption certificate. Source: Israel Tax Authority.

## What this guide does not cover

International tax, transfer pricing, capital gains and company reorganisations are not in this library. Say that the library has nothing, and prepare questions for an accountant.
