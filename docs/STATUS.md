## In progress

- **The AI assistant knows quotes, time and billing.** Ask "who still owes me money?" and it lists open balances on quotes and bills, plus quotes waiting for an answer. In industries that bill by time it can also report hours logged per client and staff member, preview the month's billing, and prepare logging time or billing the month. As with bookings, each action is a card the user confirms before anything happens, and it's written to the audit log. Demo mode (no AI key) answers "who owes me money?" and "how many hours this month?" from real data.
- **Go-live hardening, first part** ([#51](https://github.com/MyBiz-app/business-os/issues/51)). Rate limits per visitor: public forms, public pages and webhooks each have a limit, and every call has a ceiling; over a limit the API answers "too many requests". `/health/ready` checks the database for uptime monitoring. A contract test checks every registered provider against its capability's interface, so a new vendor class is checked automatically. Still to come at go-live: monitoring and alerts, a backup restore drill, the security and privacy reviews, and the store builds.
- **Billing the month for every client at once** (Professional services). A "Billing the month" page lists every client with a retainer or unbilled time, with the monthly fee, the hours worked, what goes beyond the retainer and the total. You choose who to bill and bill them all with one button. A client already billed for that month is marked and never billed twice. Checked in `e2e/practice.py`, where the demo bills 59 clients at once.
- ✅ [#98](https://github.com/MyBiz-app/business-os/issues/98) Client app, second round. The home screen has "Waiting for you": bills and deposits to pay, quotes to answer and documents to sign, each one tap away. The profile shows "In my account" as a list with badges (documents to sign, quotes and bills to pay, my receipts, addresses, pets or children, the health declaration). "My receipts" lists every payment with its receipt. A bill shows as a bill with what's left to pay. Bookings have status badges and a shorter history ("show all"). Checked end to end with `e2e/client_account.py`; the client crawl also covers receipts, quotes and documents.
- ✅ [#45](https://github.com/MyBiz-app/business-os/issues/45) Documents, retainers and time-based billing (X14, [proposal](proposals/45-documents-retainers.md)). Every client card has documents: upload (PDF, images, Word, Excel up to 10 MB, kept by the storage provider), share with the client, ask the client to sign (they sign in the app by writing their name), and the client's own papers. Industries that bill by time also get time and billing on the client card: log hours, a retainer (monthly fee, hours included, hourly rate beyond) and "bill the month", which makes a bill the client pays by its private link; billed time is locked and never billed twice. "My time" shows each staff member's last 30 days. In the client app: "My documents" (open, sign); in the business app: log time on the client card. **Professional services is open (beta)**: consultants, accountants, lawyers and agencies. The demo generator has an accounting firm (`--demo office`). Checked end to end with `e2e/practice.py`. **With this, every category in the catalog is open.**
- ✅ [#44](https://github.com/MyBiz-app/business-os/issues/44) Quotes, deposits and events (X12, [proposal](proposals/44-quotes-deposits.md)). A business writes a quote (items, quantities, prices, a deposit percentage, valid-until, an event date and place), keeps it as a draft and sends it: the client opens a private link with no sign-in, accepts it with their name (or declines), and pays the deposit there (through the business's payments provider; simulated for now) with a receipt; staff record the balance. Quotes are numbered per business, a sent quote is changed by copying it as a new version, and accepted quotes with a date are listed under Events. Quotes are on the client card in the web and the business app, and under "My quotes" in the client app. **Creative & events is open (beta)**: photographers and studios, DJs, event suppliers and small venues. The demo generator has a photography studio (`--demo events`) with 45 quotes. Checked end to end with `e2e/quotes.py`.
- **Integrations: every outside service is swappable (X13, [guide](integrations.md)).** Payments, invoicing, WhatsApp/SMS, email, AI and file storage each sit behind one interface with a built-in provider that needs no account (simulated payments, MyBiz receipts, simulated messages…). A business chooses its own payments, invoicing and messaging provider in settings → Integrations (secrets encrypted, never shown again); switching vendor is choosing another one there, and adding a vendor is one class in the API. Payments: a real provider's hosted payment page and its verified webhook complete a checkout. Invoicing: each receipt is sent to the business's invoicing provider by a job, which keeps the legal document's number and link. Messages: an outbox sent by a job through the business's provider, with retries. The MyBiz console shows the providers in use. Real vendors are connected last, at go-live.
- ✅ [#42](https://github.com/MyBiz-app/business-os/issues/42) On-site jobs (X11, [proposal](proposals/42-onsite-jobs.md)). Slices 1–2 done: an appointment service can be "at the client's address" with time to get there, which blocks the technician before each job (free times and the database both count it); clients have addresses (with floor and how to get in), managed by staff and by the client in the app; booking an on-site job picks the client's address, and the job keeps a copy of it; a job moves scheduled → on the way → in progress → done by its technician, and done checks the client in; the session page shows the address with Waze and Google Maps links and the job's progress; each technician has their jobs of the day. Slices 3–4 done: the business app has "My jobs today" (the day's jobs in order with the client, address, how to get in, when to leave, Waze, Google Maps and a call, and on my way → start → done; managers see the whole team), and the job on its session screen; in the client app, "My addresses" in the profile and booking an on-site service asks where, and the booking shows the job's progress ("the technician is on the way"). **Home & field services is open (beta)** with cleaning, air conditioning, electricians, plumbers and pest control, each with on-site starter services and travel time. The demo generator has an air-conditioning company (`--demo jobs`). Checked end to end with `e2e/onsite_jobs.py` (web), `e2e/onsite_app.py` (client app) and `e2e/jobs_app.py` (business app).
