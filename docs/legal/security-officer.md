# Data security / privacy officer

Draft for a lawyer. **To confirm: whether the law requires MyBiz to appoint one now.** Amendment 13
requires an officer in certain cases (for example, bodies that process sensitive data on a large
scale, or whose main activity is monitoring people); the thresholds should be checked against MyBiz's
size and data (health declarations, ID numbers and bank details are in the
[inventory](data-inventory.md)). Until the lawyer answers, the owner holds the role.

## Role

The officer (ממונה אבטחת מידע / ממונה הגנה על הפרטיות) is responsible for:

1. Keeping the [data security procedure](security-procedure.he.md) and the
   [inventory](data-inventory.md) up to date, at least once a year and after any big change
   (a new provider, a new kind of data).
2. Running the [annual vendor review](vendor-review.md).
3. Leading the response to a security event with the on-call owner ([on-call](../deploy/on-call.md)),
   and deciding with counsel what is reported to the Authority, the businesses and individuals.
4. Answering a business's or a person's privacy request, or checking that support did.
5. Training the team once a year (even one person: read the procedure and sign the log).
6. Reporting to the owner every quarter: incidents, requests, open risks.

Independence: the officer must not be pressed to hide a problem. While the owner is the only
person, this is a promise in writing, not a structure; revisit it when there is a team.

## Appointment text (to fill in and sign)

> I, {name}, appoint {name}, {id}, as the data security officer of {company name, registration
> number} from {date}. Contact: {phone}, {email}. The officer has access to the systems and
> records needed for the role and reports to {the owner}.

Where the contact goes once appointed: `docs/legal/security-procedure.he.md` (section 2), the
privacy policy page, the accessibility statement is separate (it has its own coordinator).
